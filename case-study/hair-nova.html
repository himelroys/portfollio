01About the Client

Hair Nova Salon isn't just a hair salon. It's a brand built on precision, style, and a genuine passion for making people feel their best. With three thriving locations across London (Finchley and Angel) and Leicester, the Hair Nova team had something powerful: a loyal client base, talented stylists, and a reputation that kept people coming back.

What they didn't have was a reliable digital system to bring in new clients consistently, every single week. Our challenge was clear: three separate branches, three separate audiences, and a need to make every pound of ad spend work harder than the last.

02Multi-Branch Strategy

Search Campaigns

Captured high-intent prospects actively searching for salon services with keywords like 'hair salon near me', 'hair extensions Finchley', and 'best salon Angel London'.

Performance Max

Leveraged Google's machine learning across Search, Display, Maps, and YouTube to maximise reach and conversions at the lowest possible cost.

Smart Local Campaigns

Built broad visibility with strong conversion signals like phone calls, direction requests, and store visits for local discovery.

03Keyword & Location Targeting

Each branch received a dedicated Google Ads account with thorough keyword research around local intent queries, paired with location-targeted campaigns set to radiate outward from each salon's postcode.

07Key Insights

Leicester: Lowest CPC on Record

Achieved a CPC of just £0.37 — the lowest across all three accounts. This demonstrates precise audience targeting and efficient campaign structure right from launch.

Limited by Budget = Opportunity

Both Finchley (Search) and Leicester (PMax) are flagged 'Limited by Budget' by Google. This is an opportunity signal: there is proven demand that additional budget can immediately capitalise on.

Angel: The Mature Benchmark

With 12 months of data, Angel is the most established account. Its blended CPA of ~£9.55 for a premium salon service represents exceptional efficiency.

Phone Calls = Highest Quality Leads

Collectively 697+ phone calls were generated — arguably the highest-quality lead type for a local salon, indicating customers are ready to book immediately.
