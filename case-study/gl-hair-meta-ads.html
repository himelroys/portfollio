01About the Project

GL Hair Extensions is a UK-based premium hair extension brand offering high-quality human hair extensions priced between £150 and £350 per unit. Operating in one of the most competitive beauty markets in Europe, they needed a consistent pipeline of qualified leads without relying solely on organic reach.

The Meta strategy was designed as the top-of-funnel engine — generating awareness, capturing interest, and funnelling warm leads toward purchase. This complemented the Google Search strategy by feeding the retargeting pool and building brand recognition before buyers entered search mode.

023-Stage Funnel Strategy

Stage 1

Cold Audience Lead Gen

Targeted UK women interested in hair care, beauty, and fashion. Highest volume bid strategy with image and carousel ads showcasing product quality and transformations.

Stage 2

Retargeting & Re-engagement

Re-engaged website visitors and social engagers with softer messaging, testimonials, and direct call-to-action. Built trust before asking for the sale.

Stage 3

Ongoing Lead Optimisation

Continuously optimised for low-cost enquiries using 7-day click attribution. A/B tested creatives, audiences, and ad copy to drive CPL down.

05Key Insights

Dual-Platform Synergy

Meta Ads built awareness and generated leads at £4.47 CPL, while Google Ads captured high-intent buyers. Together they created a full-funnel growth engine.

Retargeting is Essential

The retargeting campaign re-engaged warm audiences who had already interacted with the brand, dramatically improving conversion rates at lower cost.

Highest Volume Bid Strategy

Using Meta's Highest Volume bid strategy with 7-day click attribution maximised lead volume while keeping costs predictable.

Creative Quality Drives CPL

High-quality product imagery and transformation-focused creatives consistently outperformed generic lifestyle shots, directly impacting cost per lead.
